## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Withdrawal and returns policy content
The `#returns` section SHALL also explain the handling of uncollected or refused courier parcels, including that courier status is reviewed by Atelier Marie before refund, stock, or accounting decisions are finalized.

#### Scenario: Uncollected parcel policy is visible
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the returns section
- **THEN** the page explains that uncollected or refused parcels may require admin review before refund timing and amount are confirmed
- **AND** it distinguishes card refunds from payment-on-delivery orders where no card payment was collected

### Requirement: Return condition and return shipping disclosure
The returns section SHALL also explain that return shipping, courier return fees, or diminished-value deductions may affect the final refund amount where legally permitted and applicable.

#### Scenario: Return costs are disclosed
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the returns section
- **THEN** the page explains that return shipping or courier return costs may be reviewed before the final refund amount is confirmed
- **AND** it does not promise an automatic full refund for uncollected, refused, or returned parcels

### Requirement: Faulty, damaged, or incorrect item handling
The Terms & Conditions page SHALL also explain that courier-damaged or lost parcels may require Atelier Marie to record courier claim details, while customer statutory rights remain unaffected.

#### Scenario: Courier damage or loss language is present
- **WHEN** a visitor reads the faulty or damaged items section
- **THEN** the page explains that Atelier Marie may record courier claim details for damaged or lost parcels
- **AND** it does not require the customer to manage the courier claim directly
