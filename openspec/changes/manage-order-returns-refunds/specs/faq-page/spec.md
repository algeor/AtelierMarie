## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Public FAQ page
The FAQ content SHALL include a short delivery/returns answer for uncollected or refused parcels, localized in English and Bulgarian, and point customers to the Terms & Conditions returns section for the complete policy.

#### Scenario: FAQ explains uncollected parcels briefly
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the FAQ page
- **THEN** a published FAQ answer explains that uncollected or refused parcels are reviewed before refund or next-step decisions
- **AND** the answer links to `/[locale]/terms#returns`
